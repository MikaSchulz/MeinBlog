Moin, here I am at last! About a year late, things are finally getting started here. Oops. And I have to admit: that wasn't really the plan.

The idea is actually simple. I often just try things out, and I wanted to share that with the world. The result is this blog.

So why only now? I'm quite a perfectionist and honestly didn't know how to start. When I think of blogs, I think of pros and experts writing scientific texts or laying out best practices. That's not me. I'm just a guy from Hamburg who wants to share a few of his ideas. It took me a while to realize: I just have to start.

So let me say this up front: what I share here won't always be right or best practice. Feel free to criticize things and do them differently. I think I get a lot of things right, and when I don't, I'm happy to exchange ideas, discuss and learn together.

Since the blog has existed for almost a year without me writing anything, quite a bit has piled up. My list of topics is long, and I'll work through it step by step. Especially at the beginning, you can look forward to lots of posts. That will surely slow down a bit over time, but my goal is to write regularly.

So feel free to reach out via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mika-schulz-538b5a246/), [GitHub](https://github.com/MikaSchulz) or by email at [info@eyetealer.me](mailto:info@eyetealer.me).

A little fun fact you can probably guess already: "eyetealer" sounds like "ITler", German for "IT guy". Hence the name. ;)

In the next post, I'll write about the blog itself. To be honest, an AI built most of it, with some input from me here and there. I'll tell you how that went next time.
