Yo, here I am, finally. With a delay of around a year, I finally made it. Yeah, this wasn't actually planned. Oopsie.

So if you don't know me yet: I'm Mika from Hamburg, just a normal guy who likes to try out stuff. And I wanted to start sharing it with the world. So the idea that came to my mind was pretty simple: writing a blog.

So why such a long delay? Actually, I'm pretty much a perfectionist, and I didn't really know how to start. Because when I think about blogs, I think about experts writing scientific papers or formulating best practices. But that's not really me. I'm just a simple guy who wants to share his ideas. So it took me a while until I finally realized I should just start.

So whatever I share here doesn't have to be right or best practice. You can criticize it or do things differently, but I think I've really got a lot right already. And if not, just send me a message. Let's start a conversation, discuss it, learn from each other, and so on.

Since the blog has already existed for a year now without me writing anything, I have a huge backlog of topics I want to write about. And I'll work through it bit by bit now. Especially in the beginning, you can look forward to a lot of posts. That will probably slow down in the future, but my goal is to keep writing on a regular basis.

So if you want to get in touch with me, reach out on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mika-schulz-538b5a246/), [GitHub](https://github.com/MikaSchulz), or send me an email at [info@eyetealer.me](mailto:info@eyetealer.me).

And a small fun fact for you: in Germany, IT people are sometimes called "ITler", and "eyetealer" sounds just like that. So that's where my name came from.

All right, final words: I'll start by writing about the blog itself. Actually, most of it was built by AI. Not the content, though: I'm writing all of this myself. Okay, I'll tell you all about it in the next post. See ya!
