Yo, here I am. Finally, with a delay of around a year. Oopsie. I finally made it, even though that wasn't actually the plan.

If you don't know me yet: I'm Mika, from Hamburg, a normal guy who likes to try out stuff. And I want to start sharing that with the world. Pretty simple idea, and what came to mind was writing a blog.

So what took me so long? I'm pretty much a perfectionist, and I didn't really know how to start. When I think about blogs, I think about experts writing scientific papers or formulating best practices. But that's not really me. I'm just a simple guy who wants to share his ideas. It took me a while to realize that I should just start.

So whatever I share here doesn't have to be right or best practice. You can criticize it or do things differently. But I think I already get a lot right. And if not, I'd really like you to send me a message. Let's start a conversation, discuss it and learn from each other.

Since the blog has already existed for about a year without me writing anything, I have a huge backlog of topics I want to write about. I'll work through it bit by bit, and especially in the beginning, you can look forward to lots of posts. That will probably slow down in the future, but my goal is to keep writing on a regular basis.

If you want to get in touch, reach out on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mika-schulz-538b5a246/), [GitHub](https://github.com/MikaSchulz) or send me an email at [info@eyetealer.me](mailto:info@eyetealer.me).

Small fun fact for you: in Germany, IT people are sometimes called "ITler", and "eyetealer" sounds just like that. That's where the name comes from. ;)

Alright, final words: I'll start by writing about the blog itself. Most of it was actually built by AI, not the content though. I'll tell you all about it in the next post.
